***In progress***


As per usual, I was doomscrolling LinkedIn one day when I came across a [familiar](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/imc-trading_ready-to-put-your-trading-and-coding-skills-activity-7299779930765029376-n3h5/) [post from IMC](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/imc-trading_prosperity-4-launch-activity-7439339944646684672-LMJm/). It was time for this years annual IMC Prosperity trading challenge. I originally wasn't planning on participating as my team name from last year was [HireMePls](https://github.com/akaashdash/imc-prosperity-3) and I indeed got hired (although not because of this competition). However, one of my friends asked if I wanted to join his team and who am I to say no to a fun little competition. Despite the tortuous memories of sleepless nights and stress, I have very fond memories of learning a lot throughout the struggle and I thought that this year could be even better with a solid team and claude code by my side.

### Pre-Competiton Setup

Unfortunately jmerle was no longer able to publish his backtester and visaulizer for this year, but the open source community was kind enough to publish some adaptations. A big concern that others had with these tools is that there was high potential for somebody to steal the algo files or logs and copy the strategies. I didn't really share these concerns as I trusted that if this were to happen, the mods would ban those players and render that idea useless.
